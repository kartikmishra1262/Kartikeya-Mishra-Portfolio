Made in the dark hours

NOIRÉ began with a single question: why do most fragrances ask to be liked? We wanted one that simply arrived, quietly certain, and let the room come to it.

Our perfumers work in small batches, macerating each blend for ninety days before it is filtered, bottled and sealed by hand. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is added for effect.

The result is a house with few fragrances and no filler. Every bottle is black, every cap is gold, and every scent is meant to be remembered.
